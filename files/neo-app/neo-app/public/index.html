A smart electricity monitoring concept that helps users understand and manage consumption. Live values below are simulated.

⚡ Energy-saving suggestions

  • Switch to 5-star inverter appliances to cut standby loss.
  • Shift heavy loads (iron, pump) away from evening peak hours.
  • Replace old bulbs with LEDs — up to 80% less power.
  • Unplug chargers and set-top boxes when idle.
